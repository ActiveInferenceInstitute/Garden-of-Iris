# Introduction

Grouping a conversation by timestamp keeps ordering but says little about the role of an utterance. Garden-of-Iris adds a second orientation: predictions are directed toward future verification, reflections toward past observations, statements toward present assertions, and questions toward open inquiry. These labels make the unit of collection more explicit for downstream processing. Membership in a category remains a description of a communicative role. It gives no independent guarantee of truth, calibration or agreement.

The research object here is the repository and its checked-in artifacts. The contribution is a data-flow description that differentiates collection surfaces from model-dependent transformations and records the evidence a future evaluation would require [@source].
